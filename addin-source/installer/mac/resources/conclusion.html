Done. Two quick steps the first time:

  1. Open PowerPoint and open or create a presentation.
  2. Go to Home > Add-ins (or Insert > My Add-ins) and choose Deli Elements under Developer Add-ins. The Deli tab then appears on the ribbon.

For a new deck in the Deli look: File > New from Template, then pick Deli presentation.

To remove it later, run Uninstall Deli Elements.command in
/Library/Application Support/Deli Elements.
